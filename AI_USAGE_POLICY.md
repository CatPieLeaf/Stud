# Stud AI usage policy

Contributions that are assisted with generative AI are permitted, but we expect a human overseeing, reviewing and cleaning up the generated code. DO NOT let the AI agent co-author or author its own commits, as the contributor is expected to make and author their own commits, and be fully responsible of all the changes. If you don't understand at minimum what you're doing, please do not attempt to PR.

To ensure self-review and full understanding of the changes, it is strongly recommended to not use generative AI to write the PR description. (unless translation tools are needed)

If heavily using generative AI for your contribution, please trim comments and cleanup as much as possible your submission before opening a PR.
